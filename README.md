# Eurac Research

Eurac Research is a private research centre in Bolzano, Italy, working across alpine environment, earth observation, health and autonomy studies. Its MAPS portal publishes research datasets — snow cover, land use, climate and remote-sensing products for the Alpine region — through an OGC-standard GeoServer callable anonymously by any GIS client.

Profiled 2026-08-20 as part of the [OGC](../ogc/) standards-body pass — this organization is an
**OGC Catalyst member** (Non-Profit, Italy), found in OGC's own
active-member roster and confirmed to serve a live OGC surface on its own host.
